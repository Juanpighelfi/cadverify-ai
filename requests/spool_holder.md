# Wall-mounted spool holder on a bearing

A holder for a 1 kg filament spool that screws to the wall, the spool turning on a 608 bearing
(8 mm bore, 22 mm outside, 7 mm wide).

- A wall bracket with two countersunk holes for 4 mm wood screws, and an arm about 75 mm long
  that carries an axle for the bearing.
- A hub that fits the spool's center hole (53 mm across) and holds the bearing pressed into its
  middle; the hub turns with the spool around the arm's axle.
- The loaded spool weighs about 1.2 kg; the arm must not sag.
- PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports.
