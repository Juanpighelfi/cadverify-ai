# Case for a Raspberry Pi 4

A two-part case for a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B: a base the board screws into, and a lid that snaps
on.

- The board is 85 x 56 mm and 1.4 mm thick, with four M2.5 mounting holes 3.5 mm in from the
  edges: 58 mm apart along its length and 49 mm across.
- Openings for every port: USB-C power, two micro-HDMI and audio along one long side, the USB
  ports and Ethernet at one end, and the microSD card at the other end, reachable without
  opening the case.
- Standoffs for the screws, and vents over the processor.
- PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports.
