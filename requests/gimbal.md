# Print-in-place gimbal

A gimbal that prints as one piece: an outer ring on a small stand, a middle ring pivoting inside
it on one axis, and an inner disc pivoting inside the middle ring on the perpendicular axis.

- About 70 mm across.
- Every pivot printed in place, with clearances for PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle,
  so the rings swing free.
- The middle ring turns a full turn on its axis, and so does the inner disc, without hitting
  anything.
- No supports.
