# Planetary gear set printed in place

A planetary gear set that prints as one piece and turns right off the bed: a sun gear in the
middle, three planet gears around it, and a ring gear outside.

- About 60 mm across and 10 mm thick.
- Straight or herringbone teeth, your call, as long as the gears stay captive (they must not fall
  out of the ring) and everything prints without supports.
- Clearances that work in PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, so nothing fuses.
- A hex hole in the sun gear for a 1/4 inch bit, to drive it.
