# Crank gear train printed in place

A desk toy that prints as one piece and turns right off the bed: a crank on a base turns a train
of gears.

- A base about 100 x 60 mm with three or four spur gears of different sizes in a row, each on a
  fixed pin with a cap, so no gear can lift off.
- The first gear has a crank handle standing up from it; turning it turns every gear, each at
  its own ratio and in its own direction.
- The crank clicks at every quarter turn: a small bump on a flexible arm of the base drops into
  a notch on the first gear's hub, so you can count the turns.
- Everything printed in place, with clearances for PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle,
  so nothing fuses and every gear turns free. No supports.
