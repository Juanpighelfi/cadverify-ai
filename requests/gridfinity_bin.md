# Gridfinity bin, 2 x 1

A storage bin for my Gridfinity drawer system, 2 units by 1 unit and 3 units tall.

- The Gridfinity grid is 42 mm per unit, so the bin's outside is 83.5 x 41.5 mm (0.25 mm of
  clearance on each side); height units are 7 mm.
- The standard Gridfinity foot under each unit, so it drops into a standard baseplate: the
  stepped profile of 0.8 mm at 45 degrees, 1.8 mm vertical and 2.15 mm at 45 degrees.
- The standard stacking lip on top, so another bin stacks on it.
- A label shelf along one long side, and a scoop at the front so small parts slide out.
- PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports.
