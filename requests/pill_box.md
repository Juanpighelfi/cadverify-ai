# Weekly pill box with a turning lid

A round pill box with seven compartments, one per day, and a lid that turns over them: the lid
has one opening, and turning it lines the opening up with the next day.

- About 90 mm across and 25 mm tall.
- The lid clicks into place over each of the seven compartments (a small bump and a notch at each
  step) and holds there, so it does not turn by itself in a bag.
- The lid turns but does not lift off: a lip under its rim, snapped on when I put it together.
- PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports, walls at least 1.2 mm.
