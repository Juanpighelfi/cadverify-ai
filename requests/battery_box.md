# Holder for two AA batteries with a sliding cover

A box that holds two AA batteries side by side (each 14.5 mm across and 50.5 mm long), with a
cover that slides off along their length and clicks shut.

- Room at each end for the metal contacts: 3 mm at the positive end and 6 mm at the spring end,
  and two 2 mm holes for the wires.
- The cover slides in grooves along both sides, and a small flexible catch holds it closed.
- Walls at least 1.2 mm. PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports.
