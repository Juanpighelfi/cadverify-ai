# Desk unit with two drawers

A small desk organizer: a frame with two drawers, one above the other, each sliding out on its
own.

- Outside about 120 mm wide, 150 mm deep and 90 mm tall.
- Each drawer pulls out at least 100 mm and then stops: it must not come out by accident, but I
  want to take it out on purpose (a stop the drawer gets past when I lift or squeeze it is fine).
- A pull on the front of each drawer.
- Each part prints on its own in PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 (256 mm bed) with a 0.4 mm nozzle, no
  supports, with clearances so the drawers slide freely.

Show me the closed unit as the assembly, and each drawer sliding out as its motion.
