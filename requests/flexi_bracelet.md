# Bracelet of hinged links, printed in place

A bracelet of ten identical links joined by hinges, printed flat in one go: it comes off the bed
already linked, every joint swinging free.

- Each link about 18 mm long, 12 mm wide and 5 mm thick; the bracelet about 180 mm around.
- Each joint is a hinge printed in place: a pin on one link inside a knuckle of the next, with
  the clearance PLA needs on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, so it does not fuse.
- Each joint bends at least 30 degrees to either side, so the bracelet wraps a wrist.
- No supports, inside the joints included.
- The two end links close with a snap.
