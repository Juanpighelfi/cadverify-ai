# Jar with a screw-on lid

A storage jar with a threaded lid, like a food jar.

- Inside about 60 mm across and 70 mm deep.
- The lid screws on with a real thread, about one and a half turns from first contact to closed,
  and closes against the jar's rim.
- Threads that print without supports on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, in PETG, with the
  clearance PETG needs to turn by hand.
- Walls and floor at least 1.6 mm.
