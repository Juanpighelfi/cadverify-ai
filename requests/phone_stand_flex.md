# Desk stand for a phone, with flexible fingers

A desk stand that holds a phone in portrait on two flexible fingers, one on each side, printed in
PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle.

- One stand fits any phone from 60 to 90 mm wide, case included, with no adjustment and no part to
  change.
- Pushing a phone in takes between 3 and 15 N at each finger, for the narrowest phone and for the
  widest.
- A phone weighs up to 300 g. Someone taps the screen with about 5 N near its top edge, and the
  stand stays standing on a desk, without being glued to it.
- The fingers flex every time a phone goes in, for years: they are not close to breaking at any
  width.
- The screen and the charging port stay clear, and the cable has a way out at the back.
- As few parts as make sense, printed without supports.
