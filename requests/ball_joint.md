# Phone stand on a ball joint

A desk phone stand whose holder tilts and turns on a ball joint.

- A base about 90 mm across, a short post with a ball about 20 mm across on top, and a holder
  with a socket that snaps over the ball.
- The holder cradles a phone up to 80 mm wide and 10 mm thick, upright or sideways.
- The socket grips the ball firmly enough to hold a 200 g phone at any tilt up to 30 degrees
  from vertical, and turns all the way around.
- PLA on a Bambu Lab A1 with a 0.4 mm nozzle, no supports.
